# Book-opening sounds

Every file in this folder is original audio synthesized by `scripts/synthesize-sounds.mjs`. There is no third-party recording and no copied melody. Each book style has its own short tune. A cover effect is mixed in: a leather creak, a cloth thump, a sparkle, a paper rustle, a metal ting, a vinyl pop, a soft air pad, or a low bubble. They are not CC0 samples from anyone else.
