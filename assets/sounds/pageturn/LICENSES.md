# Page-turn sound licenses

Six real recorded book/journal page turns, trimmed to a single turn, faded, high-pass filtered, and loudness-normalized for app use. All six are CC0 (public-domain dedication). Attribution is not required. Optional credit is noted below where the author invited it.

These notes transcribe the license stated on each source page. They are not legal advice or trademark clearance.

Processing applied to every file: 50 Hz high-pass, trim to one page turn (~0.7–1.5 s), short fade in/out, peak-limited to about −1.5 dBTP, exported as 160 kbps stereo MP3. `real-3-heavy-thick-paper.mp3` also received light FFT denoise for residual hiss.

## Sources considered and not used

- YouTube: “Page Turning Sound Effect - Flip Book Pages” by Free Audio Zone (https://youtu.be/DeFmzObtgPU) was used only as a written reference for the kind of sound wanted (close-mic, realistic, slow book-page flips). The YouTube audio was not downloaded or used. The public video description says the sound is for non-commercial personal projects only, and that publishing or hosting it on other channels or websites is not allowed. That does not allow use in a commercial mobile app.
- The same description points to a paid library, Silverplatter Audio “Paper” (https://silverplatteraudio.com/products/paper, $24). That is not a free official download with a clearly granted commercial-app license, so it was not used.
- Other recordings were reviewed and skipped for quality or license reasons, including hissy or harsh takes (for example Freesound “turning hard pages” by Luke100000, which is CC-BY 4.0 but had a high hiss floor), clipped or game-like OpenGameArt flips, a pitch-shifted Wikimedia hardcover recording, and aggressive notebook rustles.
